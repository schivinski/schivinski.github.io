four and released as one batch (Bruno, 7 Nov 2026). See watch/WATCH_GUIDE.md, "The 2023 tab".

with open papers on sites that open (Bruno: skip papers that cannot be accessed and fill the gaps with other articles).
All first-online dates were confirmed on the publisher pages before release.
